FAQ

Questions, answered

Which clients are supported?

Anything that speaks the OpenAI API: SillyTavern, JanitorAI, Chub, LangChain, the official OpenAI SDKs, curl, and more. Just point it at our /v1 endpoint.

Is it really free?

Yes — the proxy is community-powered and free for members of our Discord server. Fair-use limits apply to keep things fast for everyone.

Why do I need to join the Discord server?

It's our member gate and community hub: announcements, model updates, support and feedback all happen there.

What happens if an upstream key dies?

Nothing, from your perspective. The proxy detects rate limits and errors, rotates to the next key or provider, and retries automatically.
